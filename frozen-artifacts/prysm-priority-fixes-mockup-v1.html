2

Service scope is not clear enough

Supporting priority
Visitors can reach the site, but the offer is not explained with enough clarity to support fast buyer understanding.
30 DAYS
Do

Clarify the service offer using plain-language headings, tighter category naming, and a clearer connection between buyer needs and services.

Keep

Keep the existing service breadth and strategic positioning where it already aligns with audience expectations.

Avoid

Do not add more categories or longer copy if the main issue is clarity and grouping.

Check

Verify that a first-time visitor can quickly tell what the company does, who it helps, and what next step to take.

Done when

The service scope is understandable without extra interpretation and supports a clear next action.

Governance: state ESTABLISHED · action FIX · scope assessed service messaging and offer clarity · timing 30 DAYS.
